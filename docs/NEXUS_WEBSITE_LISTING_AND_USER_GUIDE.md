## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (Public FAQs)

#### Q1: Which social media platforms does Nexus support?
Nexus natively supports scheduling and publishing directly to:
1. **YouTube Shorts**
2. **TikTok**
3. **Instagram Reels**
4. **Facebook Reels**
5. **X (Twitter)**

#### Q2: Does Nexus require an expensive cloud subscription to run?
No. Unlike cloud-based clippers that charge \$30 to \$99 every single month for limited render minutes, Nexus runs locally on your Windows PC. You can render unlimited videos, extract unlimited clips, and use free local AI models (via Ollama) or free ultra-fast cloud transcription (via Groq) with zero subscription markups.

#### Q3: How does Nexus keep my social media accounts safe?
Nexus connects directly to official platform APIs using your own developer credentials. Your Client IDs, Client Secrets, and Access Tokens are encrypted locally on your machine using military-grade AES-256 encryption. Your credentials and passwords are never transmitted to NORVI servers or any third-party database.

#### Q4: Can I run Nexus completely offline?
Yes. Nexus supports full offline operation using local Faster-Whisper models for audio transcription and local Ollama models (such as Gemma) for highlight scoring. You only need an internet connection when you want to discover YouTube videos or publish scheduled posts to social platforms.

#### Q5: What computer specs do I need to run Nexus?
Nexus is built on an ultra-lightweight PySide6 Qt desktop architecture. It runs smoothly on Windows 10 or 11 with 8GB RAM and any multi-core processor (Intel Core i3/i5/i7/i9 or AMD Ryzen). Having an NVIDIA graphics card accelerates rendering, but Nexus includes an optimized multi-threaded CPU fallback that runs seamlessly on standard laptops and desktops.

#### Q6: How does the Background Publishing Daemon work?
When you close or minimize Nexus, it rests quietly in your Windows system tray. Its background daemon monitors your SQLite database and wakes up automatically when a scheduled video time arrives. It publishes your clip, logs performance, updates your calendar status, and returns to sleep without interrupting your work.
